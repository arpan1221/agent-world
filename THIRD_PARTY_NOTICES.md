# Third-party components

- cc-topology, copyright 2026 Arpan Nookala, MIT. A copy of the headless extractor is in
  vendor/cc-topology, with its original LICENSE. The app adds a cache for unchanged files.
- Three.js r128, MIT, installed from npm. Its license ships in node_modules/three/LICENSE.
- xterm.js and addon-fit, MIT; node-pty, MIT; ws, MIT. Installed through the npm lockfile.
- Minecraft-Font by IdreesInc, SIL Open Font License 1.1. `assets/mc.otf` is the
  unmodified upstream font. The font's license is in assets/OFL.txt.
  Upstream: https://github.com/IdreesInc/Minecraft-Font

Block, mob, sky and structure textures are generated by the world itself. No Minecraft
game textures, sprites, sounds or game binaries are included.
